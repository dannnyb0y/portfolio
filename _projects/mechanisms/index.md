**Project Type** - High School

This project was a challenge to take an existing physical item and recreate it within CAD. The recreation was then 3D printed and tested for functionality compared to the original item. 

The mechanism recreated was a small device that connected a crank to a fan. Gears connected the crank to the fan created a gear ratio that allowed the fan to spin much faster than the crank was turned. 

This project was completed through the Product Design class offered by the Space & Engineering Academy at Merrill F West High School.
