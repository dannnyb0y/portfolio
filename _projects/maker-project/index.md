**Project Type** - High School

This project was an intro to designing an assembly meant to be manufactured with a laser cutter. The rocket is meant to be a decorative piece, to be placed on a desk or shelf. The project was treated like it would be sold as a set and assembly instructions were created. 

This project was completed through the Product Design class offered by the Space & Engineering Academy at Merrill F West High School.

See the [Assembly Instructions](../../assets/maker/instructions.pdf) (PDF download).

## Design
The rocket's components were designed in Onshape and exported to a laser cutter for manufacturing from acrylic.

See the [Body Drawing](../../assets/maker/body.pdf) and [Rings Drawing](../../assets/maker/rings.pdf) (PDF downloads). These were the drawings sent to the laser cutter for manufacturing.
