**Project Type** - High School

**Collaborators** - Manasvini Kala

This project involved designing and building a model truss bridge out of balsa wood. The bridge was then tested to see how much weight it could hold before failing. 

Bridges were evaluated based on both how much weight they could hold and how much material was used to construct them.

## Design & CAD
The bridge was modeled in Onshape and then constructed from balsa wood pieces held together with wood glue. Large engineering drawings were printed for use during construction.

## Testing
The bridge was placed on two solid supports, with a rectangular block placed in the center. The block in the center was torqued down until the bridge broke.
