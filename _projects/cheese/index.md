**Project Type** - UC Berkeley

**Collaborators** - [Kevin Sengsourichanh](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevin-sengsourichanh-4a4743217/), Nick Khamseh, Ja'Den Hunter

This project involved designing and building a prototype device that could be fed a block of cheese and have it be grated automatically. The project was presented at the ME126 project expo on May 1, 2026.

## Design
This device was intended for two use cases - first, for a professional chef needing to shred their own cheese while doing other tasks, and second, for a home chef (such as an elderly person) who may find it difficult to shred cheese on their own.
