This project involved designing and building a prototype device that could be fed a block of cheese and have it be grated automatically. The project was presented at the ME126 project expo on May 1, 2026.

## Design
This device was intended for two use cases - first, for a professional chef needing to shred their own cheese while doing other tasks, and second, for a home chef (such as an elderly person) who may find it difficult to shred cheese on their own.

A large goal of the project was to keep design and implementation simple to ensure reliability and functionality. The body doubled as a capture point for shredded cheese and a housing for the motor/grater mechanism. 

{% include image-gallery.html images="cad-full.png" height="400" caption="CAD of the full assembly." %}

The motor was a 12V high-torque DC motor simply wired up to a power switch that turned on/off flow of power from an outlet. This motor had a circular grater piece screwed onto it that would spin when the motor was powered. 

A weighted plate was used to press down the cheese onto the grater and cause it to be shredded. Originally, this was going to be a spring-powered mechanism, but initial prototyping proved unsuccessful and the designed was changed to a weighted plate. This plate has an array of pointed features that help hold the cheese in place as it shreds.
