**Project Type** - High School

**Collaborators** - [Manasvini Kala](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manasvini-kala-434293291/)

This project tasked students with creating and launching a custom model rocket. The rocket carried an altimeter to determine its apogee, a parachute for recovery, and a quail egg as a payload. 

## Design
The rocket was designed in OpenRocket. The nose cone for the rocket was designed in Onshape and 3D printed on a Prusa MK3.

## Testing
The rocket was equipped with a C6 motor and launched at our high school’s football field. The rocket’s parachute deployed and was recovered. The egg and rocket were checked for damage and the apogee was recorded. 

Our rocket reached an apogee of about 150 feet, and had a successful parachute deployment. The rocket sustained slight damage to the fins and the egg was slightly cracked but not broken.
